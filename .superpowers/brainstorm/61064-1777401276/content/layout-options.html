My recommendation: A (Master / Detail / Customer). Three reasons fit your "live meeting, variable pace" reality:
  • Project list always visible on the left — manager can jump (a stakeholder asks "what about X?", you click X). No back-button gymnastics.
  • Centre fits in viewport — for 90% of projects the entire review state (open risks, actions, capture, prompts) fits on screen. Variable pace works because dense projects naturally scroll within the centre column.
  • Customer pack accrues live on the right — typing the headline in project A, then project B, etc. builds the customer pack as you go. By the time the meeting ends, the pack is ready to export.
B is elegant if you'd type-everything (think Linear / Superhuman) but the team probably won't all be keyboard-fluent — the command bar adds friction. C hides too much per click for a live meeting where context-switching across panes wastes time.
